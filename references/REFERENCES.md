# Project References

Full-paper copies are intentionally not distributed in the source repository.
The pre-cleanup tag and verified private archive preserve the original local
files used during development. Retrieve public versions through the DOI,
arXiv, journal, or institutional-library links in `references.bib`.

## Accretion-Disk Stability and Slim Flows

- **Lightman & Eardley (1974):** classical radiation-pressure/viscous
  instability of disk accretion.
- **Abramowicz et al. (1988):** transonic slim disks, radial advection, and the
  advective upper S-curve branch.
- **Hirose, Krolik & Blaes (2009):** radiation-MHD evidence for thermal
  stability and stress/pressure timing.
- **Jiang, Stone & Davis (2013):** radiation-MHD thermal-stability simulations.
- **Kaur, Stone & Gilbaum (2023):** magnetically supported TDE/QPE disks and
  radiation-pressure instability suppression.

## TDE and QPE Disk Evolution

- **Shen & Matzner (2014):** spreading, fallback-fed, windy TDE disk evolution.
- **Pan et al. (2022):** disk-instability model for QPEs in GSN 069.
- **Chakraborty et al. (2026):** positive period derivative in Ansky.
- **Guo et al. (2026):** delayed UV response to Ansky X-ray QPEs.
- **Hernández-García et al. (2025):** doubled Ansky timescales in NICER data.

## Distributed Stream/Angular-Momentum Source Analogues

- **Tanigawa, Ohtsuki & Machida (2012):** radial distributions of mass and
  angular-momentum delivery to circumplanetary disks.
- **Zhu, Ju & Stone (2016):** shock-driven angular-momentum transport in
  circumplanetary disks.

The circumplanetary papers are used as structural analogues for distributed
stream deposition, not as direct black-hole minidisk closures.
